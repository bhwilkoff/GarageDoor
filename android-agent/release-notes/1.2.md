I have two garage doors. Garage Agent only knew about one. So, this
version is about the second one (and the third).

- **Every door on myQ's home screen shows up in Apple Home,** each as its
  own door with its own name. Add a door in myQ and it appears in Home on
  its own, usually within a minute.
- **Doors are matched by their name in myQ,** never by where they sit on
  the screen. If myQ ever reorders its list, the right door still moves.
  If two doors share a name, Garage Agent won't press either one, and it
  tells you why.
- **Your first door doesn't change.** Same tile, same lock-screen button,
  same automations.
- **Rename doors in the Home app** and Garage Agent keeps your names.
- **Share report** now includes an outline of myQ's screen (its
  structure, not your account), which helps if a myQ update ever moves
  things around.

Up to three doors, all in the same myQ home. I have tested one real door
plus a pretend second one; if you have two real doors, I'd love to hear
how it goes.

**To install:** open this page on your extra Android phone, tap
`garage-agent.apk`, allow the install, and open Garage Agent. Already
have it? Install this right over it. Your Apple Home pairing and settings
stay just as they are.
